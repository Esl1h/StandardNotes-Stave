# 8. Notation engines and libraries

Date: 2026-10-26

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The plugin is AGPL-3.0-or-later, so every library it bundles must allow that combination. Versions, licenses and sizes were checked on the npm registry and in the published tarballs on 2026-10-26 (sizes are gzip -9 of the files the browser loads):

- `@coderline/alphatab` 1.8.4, MPL-2.0 without the "Incompatible With Secondary Licenses" notice: renders standard notation and tablature from alphaTex and plays it with alphaSynth. Core 278 KB; worker and worklet entry points under 1 KB each. Ships the Bravura font (SIL OFL 1.1) and the Sonivox SoundFont (Apache-2.0, sf3 977 KB). Its importers are Guitar Pro 3 to 5, GPX, Guitar Pro 7 and 8, MusicXML, Capella and alphaTex; there is no MIDI importer
- `abcjs` 6.7.2, MIT: renders ABC 2.1 and plays it with its own synth. 148 KB. The synth loads its instrument samples from a CDN by default
- `verovio` 6.3.0, LGPL-3.0-or-later: higher quality engraving, as WebAssembly. 2.3 MB (3.7 MB with Humdrum)
- `chordsheetjs` 18.0.0, GPL-2.0-only

## Decision

- alphaTex with alphaTab and ABC with abcjs are the two engines of the first release
- Verovio is an optional, lazily loaded renderer for ABC, distributed unmodified with its license and a link to its source, as the LGPL asks
- ChordPro, when it comes, gets a parser of its own: GPL-2.0-only code cannot be combined with an AGPL-3.0 program
- Import covers what alphaTab reads: Guitar Pro 3 to 8, MusicXML and Capella, converted to alphaTex. MIDI import is left out
- Every bundled asset is credited in the README, and `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` is generated by the build

## Consequences

A new library goes through the same check before it is added. Fonts and SoundFonts are data under their own licenses and are listed with them.
